Problem Introduction (00:20-00:30)

Problem Introduction

Saatnya mempelajari rumus inti! Rumus umum perkalian pecahan adalah:

Time to learn the core formula! The general formula for fraction multiplication is:

\( \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \times c}{b \times d} \)

Kalikan pembilang dengan pembilang, dan penyebut dengan penyebut.

Multiply the numerator by the numerator, and the denominator by the denominator.

Berapakah hasil dari \( \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{4} \) ?

What is the result of \( \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{4} \) ?

Penjelasan: Pembilang: \( 1 \times 1 = 1 \). Penyebut: \( 2 \times 4 = 8 \). Hasilnya \( \frac{1}{8} \).

Explanation: Numerator: \( 1 \times 1 = 1 \). Denominator: \( 2 \times 4 = 8 \). Result is \( \frac{1}{8} \).

Hitunglah \( \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{4}{5} \) !

Calculate \( \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{4}{5} \) !

Penjelasan: \( 2 \times 4 = 8 \) dan \( 3 \times 5 = 15 \). Hasilnya \( \frac{8}{15} \).

Explanation: \( 2 \times 4 = 8 \) and \( 3 \times 5 = 15 \). Result is \( \frac{8}{15} \).

Latihan perkalian dasar: \( 6 \times 5 \)

Basic multiplication practice: \( 6 \times 5 \)

Penjelasan: \( 6 \times 5 = 30 \). Ini membantumu mengalikan angka dengan cepat!

Explanation: \( 6 \times 5 = 30 \). This helps you multiply numbers quickly!

Mari kita visualisasikan! Jika kita punya kotak lebar \( \frac{1}{2} \) dan tinggi \( \frac{1}{3} \), maka luasnya adalah \( \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{3} \).

Let's visualize! If we have a box with width \( \frac{1}{2} \) and height \( \frac{1}{3} \), then its area is \( \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{3} \).

Kotak hijau menunjukkan 1 bagian dari total 6 bagian (\( 2 \times 3 \)). Jadi \( \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6} \).

The green box shows 1 part out of total 6 parts (\( 2 \times 3 \)). So \( \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6} \).

Berdasarkan konsep visual di atas, jika kotak dibagi menjadi 2 kolom dan 3 baris, total ada berapa bagian keseluruhan?

Penjelasan: 2 kolom $\times$ 3 baris = 6 bagian. Ini berasal dari penyebut \( 2 \times 3 = 6 \).

Explanation: 2 columns $\times$ 3 rows = 6 parts. This comes from the denominator \( 2 \times 3 = 6 \).

Terkadang, hasil perkalian pecahan bisa disederhanakan. Contoh: \( \frac{2}{4} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{8} \). Karena 2 dan 8 sama-sama genap, kita bagi 2. Hasil akhirnya \( \frac{1}{4} \).

Sometimes, the result of fraction multiplication can be simplified. Example: \( \frac{2}{4} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{8} \). Since 2 and 8 are both even, we divide by 2. The final result is \( \frac{1}{4} \).

Application in word problems is very important. The key: understand the sentence, convert it to a math sentence, then calculate. If there is the word "of", it usually means multiplication!

Soal cerita: "Pak Budi punya 12 meter kayu. 2/3 darinya dipakai untuk membuat meja." Berapa meter kayu yang dipakai?

Word problem: "Mr. Budi has 12 meters of wood. 2/3 of it is used to make a table." How many meters of wood were used?

Penjelasan: \( \frac{2}{3} \times 12 = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{12}{1} = \frac{24}{3} = 8 \) meter.

Explanation: \( \frac{2}{3} \times 12 = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{12}{1} = \frac{24}{3} = 8 \) meters.

Explanation: \( 8 \times 8 = 64 \).

Jika Pak Budi memakai 8 meter dari 12 meter kayunya, berapa meter kayu yang tersisa?

If Mr. Budi uses 8 meters out of 12 meters of his wood, how many meters of wood are left?

  • Always simplify your answer if possible.
  • 4. Kata "dari" dalam soal cerita berarti perkalian (\( \times \)).
